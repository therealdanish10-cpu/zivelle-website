Our Story

Crafted With Care, Worn With Confidence

Founded on the belief that fine jewelry should be an intimate extension of one’s elegance, Zivelle marries traditional atelier artistry with contemporary grace.

Every necklace, ring, and earring in our collection begins with thoughtfully sourced materials: luminous organic freshwater pearls, hand-selected gemstones, and rich 18k gold detailing.

Under the creative direction of Zara, our pieces are designed not just to follow transient trends, but to be treasured heirlooms—crafted to accompany life’s most cherished celebrations and quiet everyday moments.
